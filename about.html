Experience Comfort Discover Alleppey Feel at Home

Thoughtfully designed to offer comfort, elegance and warmth, The Lillys is a perfect retreat for families, couples, friends and travellers seeking a peaceful getaway.

Unwind in beautifully crafted bedrooms, enjoy spacious and welcoming interiors, and experience the warmth of genuine Kerala hospitality. Every corner of our homestay is designed to make you feel relaxed, refreshed and completely at home.

Located just a 2-minute walk from the beautiful Vembanad Lake, The Lillys offers you the perfect opportunity to experience the natural charm of Alleppey. Discover scenic backwaters, picturesque surroundings, traditional Kerala culture and unforgettable boat cruises.

Whether you are planning a relaxing holiday, a family vacation or a special escape with your loved ones, The Lillys promises a stay filled with comfort, happiness and beautiful memories.
