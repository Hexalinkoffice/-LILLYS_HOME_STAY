AYURVEDIC WELLNESS & HEALING

Relax, Rejuvenate &
Restore Your Inner Balance.

Experience the healing traditions of Kerala with authentic Ayurvedic wellness therapies during your stay at The Lillys Homestay in Alappuzha. From relaxing herbal oil massages to traditional rejuvenation treatments, Ayurveda offers a natural approach to relaxation and well-being.

🌿 Detoxification ⚖️ Weight Loss ✨ Pain Management 🌸 Rejuvenation 🕊️ Stress Relief
01 · Herbal Oil Massage

Abhyanga

A classic whole-body massage with warm medicated herbal oils to improve circulation, relieve fatigue, and calm the nervous system.

02 · Mind & Relaxation

Shirodhara

Continuous rhythmic flow of warm therapeutic herbal oils over the forehead. Renowned for relieving stress, sleeplessness, and mental fatigue.

03 · Herbal Pouch Therapy

Kizhi

Warm poultices filled with medicinal herbs applied over the body. Highly effective for pain relief, stiff joints, and sore muscles.

04 · Rice Rejuvenation

Njavarakizhi

Luxurious traditional massage using medicated Njavara rice cooked in milk and herbal broth to nourish tissues and restore natural glow.

05 · Signature Detox

Panchakarma

Complete natural cleansing and detox treatments designed to re-establish mind-body equilibrium and boost overall vitality.
